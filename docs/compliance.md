# Compliance and ethics

This system operates on a public platform owned by a third party, in a regulated jurisdiction, on behalf of a Malaysian business entity. Compliance is not optional and not an afterthought.

## Meta Platform Terms

The system is designed against the [Meta Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms) and the [Community Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards). The relevant constraints we operate under:

- **No automation on personal accounts.** Pages only, always.
- **No mass identical content.** Persona divergence enforces this.
- **No engagement bait.** Replies must be substantive.
- **No deceptive identity.** Each Page is openly registered to Pendakwah Teknologi via Business Portfolio.
- **No off-platform redirection in violation of platform rules.** No DM-pushing.
- **Honour rate limits.** Throttling is calibrated to stay well under documented and undocumented thresholds.

## App posture

The Meta App backing this system is registered as a Business app in Development mode. We do not request permissions we do not need. We have not pursued App Review for permissions we do not require for our scope, on the principle that the smallest permission surface is the safest one.

Permissions in active use:
- `pages_show_list`
- `pages_read_engagement`
- `pages_manage_posts`
- `pages_manage_engagement`
- `business_management`

## Disclosure stance

We do not advertise on every reply that the comment was generated by an AI agent. We also do not deny it when asked sincerely. The architecture, the agent roster, and the existence of the system are publicly documented (this repository, our website, and the article linked from the README). Anyone who wants to know can know.

This is a defensible middle position under current Malaysian law and the EU AI Act's transparency requirements for AI systems interacting with humans. We monitor regulatory developments in both jurisdictions.

## Data handling

The system processes only data already public on the principal Page. It does not scrape private content, does not use Graph API endpoints requiring user consent beyond Page admin scope, and does not retain user-identifiable data beyond what is required for deduplication.

Stored data:
- comment IDs (opaque strings, no personal data)
- thread IDs (opaque strings, no personal data)
- per-Page health metadata
- aggregate counters

Not stored:
- comment text after processing
- user names
- user profile information
- any private message content

The processed comment-ID cap (a fixed maximum, oldest-first eviction) is in part a privacy decision: we keep just enough state to deduplicate, and no more.

## Ethical commitments

These are ours, not Meta's.

- **No deception about professional matters.** When a user genuinely needs legal, medical, or financial guidance, our agents refuse and redirect, regardless of how the question is framed.
- **No emotional exploitation.** Hidayah's empathetic mode is bounded. She does not simulate friendship for engagement.
- **No political alignment.** Agents do not endorse parties, candidates, or contested political positions.
- **No identity theft.** Each agent is a registered Pendakwah Teknologi Page, openly affiliated with us.
- **No engagement manipulation through controversy.** Hakimah engages with dismissiveness, but never amplifies it for reach.

## Incident response

If a violation occurs — a genuine content failure, a Meta enforcement action, a user complaint — we follow this sequence:

1. Pause the affected agent immediately.
2. Investigate from logs.
3. Determine whether the failure is a rule-set error, a classifier error, or a model error.
4. Fix the underlying cause; never patch the symptom.
5. Document the incident in our internal post-mortem log.
6. Resume only after human review confirms the fix.

We have not had a public incident as of this writing. The internal `error 368` events on day one are documented in [`docs/war-stories/day-1-the-368-incident.md`](war-stories/day-1-the-368-incident.md) and were detected and contained before any user impact.
